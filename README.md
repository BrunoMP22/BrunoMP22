## 🧠 About me

I help businesses **stop wasting hours on manual reports**.

I build **Power BI dashboards** and **Python automations** that pull data from Excel, SQL Server and ERP systems, so you see your numbers clearly, without copying and pasting between spreadsheets.

My background is hands-on corporate BI: writing and tuning **T-SQL** queries, building **Power BI / DAX** dashboards and **SSRS** reports, and cleaning up inconsistent data coming from ERP systems (TOTVS Protheus).

### 🧭 [Inactive Customer Reactivation Platform](https://github.com/BrunoMP22/clientes-limbo)

A distributor had thousands of customers who stopped buying, and sales reps didn't know where to start.

This web app ranks every inactive customer by **how easy they are to reach** (who opens emails, who replies), runs an email/WhatsApp follow-up cadence and only sends field reps to customers who already showed interest.

Includes a clickable BI panel with filters, a reactivation funnel and CSV export. Demo data is fully fictitious.

**Stack:** `Python` `Flask` `React` `SQL` `BI`
